<blockquote class="quote-alt"><p>December 7th, 2011</p><p><strong>Celeste</strong></p></blockquote><p>“Hi, I’m here to get some information about Reagan Mitchell, please?”&nbsp;</p><p>Celeste’s usually calm, confident voice was frantic as she approached the reception desk of the Aldersley Memorial Hospital Emergency Department. Her hands were shaking and she pressed her palms against the counter in an attempt to show that she was put together, even when she felt <em>anything </em>but calm. Inside her chest, her heart was furiously pounding. In moments, the Witch was sure it would burst through her ribcage.&nbsp;</p><p>“I’m sorry,” the woman behind the counter, a young Were, quipped in a voice that was far too cheery for the environment they were in. “What did you say the name was?”&nbsp;</p><p>“Reagan,” Celeste’s voice cracked at the word. She swallowed and tried again. “Reagan Mitchell. Her mother sent me- we were told-”&nbsp;</p><p>“Are you family?”&nbsp;</p><p><em>I hate this question.&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>How do you define family?&nbsp;</em></p><p>“Not legally but-”&nbsp;</p><p>“I’m sorry, I can only give information to family.”&nbsp;</p><p>The instant the words left her lips, the woman went back to typing on her computer as if Celeste was dismissed from being a problem she had to deal with. The Witch was typically a patient person, or at least she considered herself to be. In this moment, it was quickly waning.&nbsp;</p><p>“Listen-”&nbsp;</p><p>“Was there something else I can help you with?” She asked with a tilt of her head. Her earrings, small, bright red jingle bells, rang with the motion. Celeste had never hated a single soul more.</p><p>“What’s your name?”</p><p>“Melody.”&nbsp;</p><p>“Great. Hi, Melody. My name is Celeste Nord, I’m the High Priestess of the Northern Coven. I’m looking for information on Reagan Mitchell, I’m-”&nbsp;</p><p>“I can <em>only </em>give information to family.”&nbsp;</p><p>“I understand that, but I’ve been dating her mother for twelve years. She <em>is </em>family. I <em>am </em>family. Just not lega-”&nbsp;</p><p>“I understand that, but I can <em>only </em>give information to family.”&nbsp;</p><p>Another jingle of the goddamn earrings. It took everything Celeste had to not lean over the desk and rip them from her ears. Logically, she <em>knew </em>this was policy and knew the rage she felt wasn’t due to the Were following the requirements of her job. She could understand policies. She could even respect them, under normal circumstances.</p><p><em>But not now. Not when my daughter is the one laying in the hospital bed.&nbsp;</em></p><p>“Please, can you give me <em>anything</em>? Is she breathing on her own? Who is her doctor?”&nbsp;</p><p>“When her parents get here, I’d be happy to talk to them, and I’m sure they’ll give you information.”&nbsp;</p><p><em>But I am her parent.&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>“</em>Please…”&nbsp;</p><p>“I’m <em>really </em>sorry,” Melody replied in her voice that was too cheery for the situation. The Were smiled at her, and she couldn’t return it. “It’s just-”&nbsp;</p><p>“Policy, I know. If… if it’s possible, can you put through to her medical team a request for Dr. Jackson Aldersley to be her-”&nbsp;</p><p>“I can <em>only </em>give-”&nbsp;</p><p>“Yes, I know.”&nbsp;</p><p>Celeste stepped back away from the desk and walked across the waiting room to drop in a chair. There, she joined the crowd of people waiting to have their own medical needs met. A broken arm sat across the lobby. Another family was with a child who had clearly cracked her head open. A heavily pregnant woman was being fetched by one of the nurses to be taken back. Beside her, her partner looked elated.&nbsp;</p><p>Celeste wondered if any of them were here for the same reason she was. She sincerely hoped not.</p><p><em>“Celeste, I-”&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>Cordelia’s voice on the other end of the phone made Celeste sit all the way up. She had been lounging in bed and excitedly waiting her end of the night phone call from her girlfriend. Typically, they would end the evening together. Tonight, Cordelia was in Portland for a meeting with another Coven. She wasn’t scheduled to return for a few days. If it weren’t for her own duties with her Coven, she might have made the trip with her.&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>“Cordy, my love, what is it?”&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>“It’s Reagan she-”&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>Cordelia’s sob on the other end of the phone shattered Celeste’s heart. She wished, desperately, that she could pull her through the phone and into her arms. She didn’t know what it was that had happened, but dread filled the Witch.&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>“What is it?”&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>“They said… she took a whole bottle of pills… she’s… Celeste she tried-”&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>“Where is she?” Celeste was already on her feet and pulling on her clothes.&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>“They were taking her to Aldersley Mem-”&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>“I’m on my way. Where’s Dominic? Can he get you here sooner than driving?” Celeste asked as she pulled her sweater on over her head. She didn’t know the Warlock well, but his teleportation ability was one she remembered.&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>“I’m trying to get ahold of him but-”&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>“Keep calling. I’ll go to the hospital and be with her. Don’t try to drive. Not while you’re upset. Please. We’ll fly you here if we have to.”&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>“Okay. Celeste-”&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>“I know,” She paused and ran her fingers through her hair. She knew exactly what Cordelia was feeling, because she felt it in her own chest, too. “I know. But she’s strong. She’ll be alright.”&nbsp;</em></p><p>Celeste pulled her phone from her pocket and glanced to the texts. All from Cordelia, all stating that she hadn’t been able to get ahold of Dominic yet. Normally, the Witch was indifferent to the Warlock who Reagan adored as her closest friend. Now, when she desperately needed him to answer and get Cordelia here, she had never hated him so much.&nbsp;</p><p><em>I love you. Let me try to find out about a flight, </em>Celeste typed in reply. She hit send and scrolled through her contacts. When she found the name she was looking for, she hit dial and lifted the phone to her ear. At nearly one in the morning, she wasn’t optimistic for an answer. To her surprise, the phone clicked on and the voice on the other end made her let out a sigh of relief.&nbsp;</p><p>“Celeste? Are you okay?” Elyse Aldersley’s voice was heavy with sleep, but alert and concerned nonetheless.&nbsp;</p><p>“Your Majesty, I-” Celeste ran her fingers through her hair. She tugged at her brown locks in some attempt to compose herself. “I need a few favors.”&nbsp;</p><p>“Anything, Celeste. Name it.”&nbsp;</p><p>On the other end of the phone, she heard the Fae Queen’s murmur to another. She heard the unmistakable sounds of the woman sitting up in her bed. Celeste hated to wake her. She hated to ask for the things she was about to ask, and yet she knew if the roles were reversed, she would be bending over backwards to do the same things she was about to ask of her.&nbsp;</p><p>“Elyse… Reagan tried to kill herself.”&nbsp;</p><p>“Is she okay?”&nbsp;</p><p>“I don’t know,” Celeste’s voice was far more quiet and shaky than she preferred to hear herself. She couldn’t help it. “They won’t tell me anything. I’m not family, and Cordelia is in Portland with the Coven up there…”&nbsp;</p><p>“I can get a helicopter to her. It will be faster than trying to figure out a plane situation at this hour. Can she make it to the airport?” Elyse asked.&nbsp;</p><p>Celeste heard the sounds of Elyse getting dressed. She already had undying amounts of respect for the Fae Queen, but in this moment she had <em>never </em>loved her more. She hated to ask this of Elyse, and yet she didn’t even have to ask. Elyse’s kind heart gave it willingly.</p><p>“Yes, I think so. I can arrange for a car. I didn’t want her driving…”</p><p>“If it were Jo or Jackson, I’d feel the same way. Which hospital?”&nbsp;</p><p>“Yours. Aldersley Memorial.”&nbsp;</p><p>“I’ll be right there,” Elyse replied. “Are you alright? Do you need to stay on the phone with me?”&nbsp;</p><p>“No, I’m okay,” She murmured. “Elyse, you don’t have to come.”&nbsp;</p><p>“Like hell,” the Fae Queen said firmly. In these moments, she appreciated her forcefulness. “If it were either of my daughters, you’d be there doing the same.”&nbsp;</p><p>“Thank you, Your Majesty…”&nbsp;</p><p>“None of that,” Elyse murmured. “I’ll be there in a few minutes, and I’ll get you back to your girl.”&nbsp;</p><p>Celeste hung up the phone and placed it in her lap before she dropped her head into her hands. She needed to call Cordelia, but the words wouldn’t come. The only thing she could think was that their daughter was back behind the doors the receptionist guarded, and that she had no idea what state the girl was in.</p><p>In the twelve years she had been dating Cordelia, her love for her daughter grew and grew. She considered Reagan to be just as much <em>her</em> daughter as she was Cordelia’s. In many, many conversations with Reagan, she knew that the young Witch felt the same. Celeste adored Reagan, and Reagan gave her the same love in return.</p><p>Despite their many years of dating, they weren’t married. It wasn’t that they didn’t want to be. They did, more than anything, but the circumstances were… complicated. Celeste was loyal to the Northern Coven and had been the High Priestess for nine years. Cordelia, in return, was the High Priestess of the Eastern Coven and came from a long line of Mitchell women who had run the Coven since the beginning of Greenborough.</p><p>Though neither of them cared, their government roles made things difficult. They were already complicated enough without the titles of wife.</p><p>Though in moments like this, she wished she had it.</p><p><em>Elyse just called me, </em>her phone stated as it lit up a text with Cordelia’s name. <em>She’s sending a car to take me to the airport. I’ll be there in about two hours.</em></p><p>Celeste smiled at her phone. Her fingers were combed through her bangs as she sat up a little more. Elyse Aldersley truly thought of everything. None of the women she was helping tonight were Fae or her responsibility as Fae Queen, but that didn’t change her kindness or her generosity.</p><p><em>Thank god. Bless the Queen. I’ll see you soon. I love you, </em>Celeste replied.</p><p>She smiled at her phone for a moment before she clicked it off and pulled her knees up to her chest. It was uncomfortable in the cramped, hard backed chairs, but she needed to hold herself together the best she could. She needed <em>something</em>, but the too-cheery receptionist would give her <em>nothing</em>. Even in all the power she wielded as a leader of this city, she was powerless when it came to things like this.</p><p><em>“My mom likes you,” Reagan’s tiny voice stated in a voice that seemed loud in the hushed tones of City Hall, even if it was barely above a whisper. At seven years old, Reagan Mitchell exuded confidence far beyond her years.</em></p><p><em>“Does she now?” Celeste chuckled as she leaned back in her chair and eyed the tiny Witch.</em></p><p><em>Often, Council meetings occurred during the time that the child spent in school. Today, Reagan’s school was closed for a teacher workshop, and Cordelia’s babysitter had fell through at the last minute. So, their meeting was joined by the future leader of the Eastern Coven.</em></p><p><em>Though she didn’t dare say it aloud, Celeste thought the seven year old had much better ideas than some of the other leaders, namely the Western Coven and the Were packs. She knew she wasn’t the only one to think that, though as merely the Ambassador to the Northern Coven, she didn’t dare make that opinion known.&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>“She does,” Reagan replied in a tone that stated the opinion was obvious.&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>To the child, it seemed like the fact was just as clear as the sky being blue or the existence of cats, which Celeste knew was Reagan’s current favorite animal based on the coloring book she was currently working on. This morning, when she had sat down between Celeste and Cordelia, the child had proudly stated it was her assignment for the day. While Celeste diligently took notes on behalf of her own High Priestess, Reagan mimicked her position and colored intensely.&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>It would be a lie for Celeste to say that she wasn’t absolutely charmed by Reagan Mitchell. And her mother, if she was being completely honest with herself.&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>“I like your mom too,” Celeste replied as she nudged pieces of her salad with her fork. They had just broken for lunch, and Cordelia had stepped out to have a conversation with her own Ambassador. Rather than making the child join her, Celeste offered to eat her lunch in the Council chambers with her. “She’s very good at what she does.”&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>“No, not like that,” Reagan sighed, lifting her eyes from her picture. “She likes you like I like Amelia Aldersley. Like I want to marry her one day.”&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>“Your mom wants to marry me one day?” Celeste smirked. She set her fork down to lean forward on the table. An amused smile was given to the child, who looked up at her with a matching smirk.&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>“Duh.”&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>“Good to know,” Celeste mused. “And what do you think about that, Reagan?”&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>“I think that Reagan Aldersley is a cool name,” She replied. She set down her blue crayon and eyed Celeste. “And… also that it would be cool to have two moms. Amelia has two moms. And a dad, but I don’t really need a dad.”</em></p><p><em>“And what do you think I should do with this information?”&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>“Ask her on a date, duh.”&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>“Maybe I will.”&nbsp;</em></p><p>The touch to her shoulder pulled Celeste from her thoughts. Dark eyes trailed upward to follow the touch. Elyse Aldersley’s smile was gentle. She held her arms out, and Celeste stood to wrap her arms around the Fae Queen. Instantly, she was comforted by the touch of her friend. Elyse’s grip was tight as they hugged, and she didn’t loosen until Celeste pulled back enough to meet the Queen’s gaze.</p><p>“Have they let you see her?” Elyse asked.&nbsp;</p><p>She held on to Celeste’s arms and gently rubbed them. The touch grounded the Witch. As always, Elyse knew what to do. Celeste envied her natural ability to hold herself at <em>all </em>times. In the Council chambers or leading the Coven, she could handle herself. Outside of it, especially in moments of crisis, she was sometimes floundering.&nbsp;</p><p>“Not yet. They said family only.”&nbsp;</p><p>“Is Jackson on the case? I tried to page him, but I didn’t hear back yet.”&nbsp;</p><p>“I don’t know,” Celeste sighed and ran her fingers through her bangs once more. “They wouldn’t tell me a damn thing.”&nbsp;</p><p>“Come on.”&nbsp;</p><p>Elyse took her hand and pulled her over to the doors near the reception desk. Melody lifted her gaze from her computer just as Elyse paused to look through her bag.&nbsp;</p><p>“Ma’am, you can’t just-”</p><p>“Watch me,” Elyse replied. A hospital badge was pulled from her purse and pressed against the keypad. The doors swung open, and Celeste let out a sigh of relief. “I own the damn hospital, and I am going to get a mother to her child now.”</p><p>“I’m sorry I didn’t-”&nbsp;</p><p>“They’ve been separated long enough. What room?”&nbsp;</p><p>“115.”&nbsp;</p><p>“Thanks.”</p><p>Celeste envied Elyse’s ability to command a room even more in situations like this one. She felt her hand be gripped tighter as she was pulled through the doors and down the long hallway towards her daughter. With every step, she felt her chest tighten. She wished, more than anything, that she had some indication of what she was walking into.&nbsp;</p><p>Elyse turned the knob and gently nudged Celeste into Reagan’s room. Her eyes were kind and calm. Celeste knew she had the appearance of someone who was seconds away from completely losing her own composure. She hesitated for just a moment, terrified of what she might see.&nbsp;</p><p>“I’m here. I’m with you,” Elyse murmured.&nbsp;</p><p>“I know,” Celeste whispered.&nbsp;</p><p>She stepped through the doorway. Her eyes immediately fell on the girl in the bed and her heart sank. She was on her side with blonde hair falling into her face. An IV was pumping fluids into her body via the needle in her arm. Reagan was pale and hooked up to a variety of wires that monitored her vitals, but she was <em>alive</em>. She was in an awful state, but the steady rise and fall of her chest told her that she was okay.&nbsp;</p><p>“Oh, Rae,” Celeste whispered. She sat down on the stool next to the bed and took her daughter’s hand. It was cold as ice, and she instantly began to rub it in an attempt to warm her.&nbsp;</p><p>“I’m going to go find Jackson and make sure he’s on her case, okay?” Elyse whispered as she put a hand on Celeste’s shoulder.&nbsp;</p><p>“Okay, thank you,” Celeste nodded, her gaze not breaking from Reagan as Elyse slipped from the room.&nbsp;</p><p>She reached up to lightly brush Reagan’s blonde hair from her eyes. In this state, she reminded her more of a child than she had ever been before. She looked so small and fragile. In all her years of knowing the Witch, she had never seen her look so tiny or frail.&nbsp;</p><p>“Momma?” Reagan’s voice was a croak. Her blue eyes fluttered open slightly, though Celeste could tell she was still very out of it.&nbsp;</p><p>“Hi Angel,” She whispered. “Your Mom is on her way.”</p><p>Celeste leaned down to give her a kiss on the forehead before she continued to stroke her hair. Her movements were slow and steady in an attempt to calm her. It reminded her of all the times Reagan had a fever and would curl up with her head on her lap.&nbsp;</p><p>“You’re… my mom,” Reagan managed. Her eyes closed again, but she felt the grip to her hand tighter than ever.&nbsp;</p><p>“And you’re my kid, Rae. But your <em>other </em>mom is on her way.”&nbsp;</p><p>“Okay.”&nbsp;</p><p>“I love you, Rae,” Celeste murmured. She squeezed her hand again gently. “More than the planets and the stars.”&nbsp;</p><p>“All… to Pluto…” Reagan murmured.&nbsp;</p><p>“All the way to Pluto,” Celeste whispered. “And then on to the very edges of the Milky Way.”</p><p>Her eyes remained closed, but the corner of Reagan’s lip curled up into a smile. Celeste smiled at her daughter and lightly pressed another kiss to her temple. This time, she let it linger. Quiet, nearly imperceptible words were muttered under her breath. A spell for strength. Reagan would need it.&nbsp;</p><p>“Momma?” Reagan whispered. Her voice was barely audible.&nbsp;</p><p>“Yes, my love?”&nbsp;</p><p>“I’m so sor…”&nbsp;</p><p>“Shh,” Celeste whispered and squeezed her hand. “You have no reason to apologize. You’re safe, and we’ve got you now.”&nbsp;</p><p>“I love… you…”&nbsp;</p><p>“And I love you, Rae,” She reached with the hand not gripping Reagan’s to pull the blankets up and tuck them around her. “Let yourself rest. I won’t leave your side.”&nbsp;<br><br></p>
