<blockquote data-alt="true" class="quote-alt">July 6, 2022</blockquote><p><strong>Adria</strong></p></blockquote><p><em>Her eyes.</em></p><p>Adria was laying in bed and could think of nothing else but the eyes of the girl from the beach. No matter how hard she tried, she kept seeing them over and over again in her mind. Even from far away, she could see how bright and beautifully blue they were. They looked like a clear summer sky. She couldn’t help but wonder what they would look like up close. It was too bad that she wouldn’t get the chance to see them again. Greenborough was huge and the shoreline was long. The odds of seeing her again were slim, if not impossible.</p><p>The Witch remembered that she was splashing around in the water, completely clothed, when she felt the tug in her chest. It was that sensation that had her lifting her eyes from Charlie who joined her, a rare occasion. She remembered scanning the beach to where the strange tugging sensation seemed to be guiding her. It pulled her to <em>those eyes</em>. Every bit of oxygen that filled her lungs was suddenly sucked from them. The world seemed to blur. All she could see with complete clarity were those blue eyes as they locked with her own. Everything else fell away, even the ocean that she loved with every bit of her. Nothing had ever felt that way before and she couldn’t look away.</p><p>The sudden knock on her bedroom door pulled her from her thoughts. Ocean blue eyes lifted to the door as it was pushed open and she saw Charlie smiling at her.</p><p>“I just wanted to say goodnight.”</p><p>“Goodnight, C,” Adria murmured as she easily returned her sister’s smile.</p><p>“You’re still thinking about her, aren’t you?” Charlie asked as she leaned against the doorframe with her arms folded across her chest.</p><p>“Yes.” Adria felt the heat rush into her cheeks and she gave a small nod. “I can’t think of anything else.”</p><p>“Creeper,” Charlie chuckled.</p><p>“Are you telling me that you haven’t been thinking about your mysterious surfer boy all night?”</p><p>“I have no idea what you’re talking about.”</p><p>“Uh-huh. Sure, Charlie. You think you’re too cool for that, don’t you?” Adria teased. She could tell that her sister was still thinking about him. The look in her eyes gave it away. Eyes that were the same shade as Adria’s own, which never ceased to amaze her. “I’m calling your bluff.”</p><p>“You do that, sis. I love you.” With that, Charlie pressed a gentle kiss to the Witch’s head. “Get some sleep.”</p><p>“Yes ma’am.” Adria replied with a playful salute as Charlie made her way to the door.</p><p> She gave a small wave as the door closed behind her sister. She sunk into her bed a bit more and pulled the blanket to her chin. No matter what Adria was always cold, even on the hottest days of summer. Her eyes slipped to the window. The city lights gave her room a strangely comforting glow. It didn’t take long for her mind to go right back to the girl and the eyes that took her breath away. Logically, she knew that nothing would ever come of the moment. Even so, something told her it would stick with her forever.</p><p><em>Not that forever is very long for me.</em></p><p>The thought made her shiver and she closed her eyes to focus on the way her heart was beating. It seemed strong, but the reality was that it was getting weaker with each passing day. Adria knew she didn’t have much time left in the world. It was something she had accepted a long time ago. She always told herself, everyone else, and especially Charlie that there was no point in getting sad because nothing would change the inevitable. Plus, she promised herself years ago that she would enjoy every moment she had left. Tonight, her typical positivity was slipping farther away as those eyes burned into her mind. They were a permanent part of her now. She wasn’t sure why something so small and seemingly insignificant was making her feel this way.</p><p><em>My fucking heart.</em></p><p><em>Those eyes.</em></p><p><em>You’ll see her again.</em></p><p>Adria wasn’t sure whose voice it was in her mind. It wasn’t one she recognized. She should have been worried, but there was a comfort in the unknown voice.</p><p><em>Don’t give up hope yet, Adria.</em></p><p>A small smile appeared on her lips as she slipped slowly into the arms of blissful sleep.</p>
