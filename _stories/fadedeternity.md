<blockquote class="quote-alt"><p>December 21, 1837</p><p><strong>Josephine</strong></p></blockquote><p>No matter how hard she tried, Josephine didn’t get one bit of sleep. It wasn’t surprising considering she had shown up at her brother’s door at nearly three in the morning. Instead, she just curled up under the blankets and stared out the window wondering what would happen when Eilian woke up to find Josephine gone with her letter on the pillow where her head should have been.</p><p>Even now, she felt like she was falling apart. With every breath, she felt as if a knife was piercing her heart. On the other side of the knife was Eilian. Josephine didn’t know that loving someone could hurt so much. For a moment, she wondered why anyone would want love in their life if this is what it led to. Quinton, her brother and her twin, seemed to be the exception. He was sleeping beside the woman he loved. They deserved every bit of the happiness that their love brought them.</p><p>Happiness that would never be Josephine’s again.</p><p>Josephine couldn’t help but wonder what she had done to end up shattered like this. Josephine was a whisper of who she was before Eilian broke the <em>only</em> promise that had ever been asked of her. She had accepted that her Queen was falling in love with Jackson. It killed her little by little. At least, before Eilian accepted the proposal, she still had the woman she loved. Josephine’s only request had been for Eilian to speak to her before she accepted. It seemed her Queen didn’t find Josephine worthy of fulfilling the promise as she was now engaged to the Hunter.</p><p><em>It was only a matter of time before she was done with you, Josephine.</em></p><p>She didn’t know whose voice was in her mind. It pulled the sob she had been trying so hard to keep in out of her. Josephine pressed a hand over her mouth to keep herself quiet. She didn’t need Quinton or Anna leaving the warmth of their bed for her. Her pain-filled eyes focused on the world outside of her window as the sun rose into the sky.</p><p><em>Will it always feel like this?</em></p><p><em>Yes.</em></p><p><em>I would rather die.</em></p><p><em>You can’t.</em></p><p>Josephine remained in the room upstairs until nearly midday when Quinton came in and insisted she joined them for lunch. It was useless to fight, so she got up and put on the dress she arrived in. As was requested, she made her way downstairs just in time to hear the knock. Her eyes shot to the door and widened before they fell on Quinton and Anna. The pull in her chest told her who it was. She felt herself falling apart all over again. <em>Eilian.</em></p><p>The Fae was rooted in place. Every bit of her strength slipped away as she watched Anna open the door. It was only when Quinton took the Witch’s place that she was able to make her way down the rest of the stairs. Her brother stepped onto the porch and pulled the door closed behind him. Josephine’s eyes met Anna’s and she could see the worry in them.</p><p>“Josephine…” Anna started as Josephine stepped past her and silently pulled the door open a crack. She watched as the Fae pressed her back to the wall and focused on the conversation between her brother and their Queen.</p><p>“I <em>need</em> to see her, Quinton.”</p><p>Josephine could hear the sadness and desperation in Eilian’s voice, and her heart ached. Even now, she hated the very <em>thought</em> of the woman she loved being in any sort of pain.</p><p>“She’s hurt. She said you accepted his proposal.”</p><p>“I did.”</p><p>Pain. Only pain.</p><p>“Why? Why would you do that?”</p><p>“I love him.”</p><p>The tears started streaming down her cheeks at those words and she pressed her hand over her heart. Somehow, she kept her crying quiet. Josephine didn’t want to see the way Anna was looking at her. She let her eyes fall closed as she listened to the conversation.</p><p>“And Josephine? Do you love Josephine?”</p><p>“I love her endlessly. She is everything to me.”</p><p>Josephine wanted to believe the words. <em>Desperately.</em> They were pretty words, but she knew they were meaningless. They were a lie.</p><p><em>They aren’t a lie, she loves you.</em></p><p><em>She has a horrid way of showing it.</em></p><p>Every second she listened, she felt her strength slipping further away. She loved Eilian. She would do <em>anything</em> for her, even if it meant Josephine would become nothing but a shell of herself.</p><p>“Eilian, please don’t ask me to choose between my loyalty to my Queen and my loyalty to my sister.”</p><p>Quinton’s words surprised her. There were vows he needed to uphold and a command from Josephine to continue protecting the Queen. For now, she was still in charge of the Knights. She had a feeling she wouldn’t hold that position much longer. Perhaps Jackson would take her place.</p><p>“If you go in there, you’re going to ask her to stay, aren’t you?”</p><p>Quinton’s question had an obvious answer: one that every one of them knew. They knew what Eilian would do. She would ask her to stay despite breaking her promise. The Queen knew Josephine would remain at her side if she was asked. Her fingers wrapped around the doorknob as she anxiously waited for her Queen’s answer.</p><p>“I know-”</p><p>Josephine’s grip tightened.</p><p>“So she would stay, and watch as you marry him. She would watch as you built a life with him. Maybe even have children. She would stay, silently at your side, and with every passing second, my sister would slip away. She would stay for her love for you, and completely lose herself in the process. Eilian, I know you love her, but I don’t think you see how much you’ve <em>shattered</em> her over the last year.”</p><p>Her hand slid away from the door as she tried to hold in the sob that was threatening to give her away. Every word that was spoken by her brother was the truth, and yet she tried to fight it.</p><p>“If you love her, Eilian, you have two choices.”</p><p><em>She is the Queen, Quinton. It isn’t your place to give her choices. Even if it has to do with me, I am much less than our Queen.</em></p><p>Josephine couldn’t breathe as the choices were given. She closed her eyes as if that would somehow ease the agony.</p><p>“Marry him and let her go, or end it with him and choose her.”</p><p>Hope began to bloom in her chest as she waited for her Queen’s response. Eilian had chosen her so many times before. Perhaps she would again. The silence was loud. All she could hear was the furious beating of her own heart. With each agonizing second of silence, her hope slipped away.</p><p><em>Please, Eilian, choose me.</em></p><p>“I’m going to marry him.”</p><p>The answer shattered her. She managed to push the door closed just in time to conceal the sob she could no longer contain. Josephine didn’t know it was possible for her heart to shatter any further. Somehow, it was. Every bit of oxygen was ripped from her lungs as she slowly sank to the floor. Her strength slipped away. All it had taken was one sentence from her Queen. The woman who was meant to be her eternity had tossed her aside.</p><p>She was no longer the girl who weaved flower crowns while she watched <em>her</em> Queen dance in the water. Gone was the woman who believed in an infinite future at Eilian’s side. Everything Josephine once believed to be true had faded with one singular sentence. No matter how many days remained of her eternal life, she would carry Eilian in her heart, but the woman the Fae Queen loved was gone.&nbsp;</p><p><em>Her</em> Josephine was gone.</p>
