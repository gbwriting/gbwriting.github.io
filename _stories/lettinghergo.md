<blockquote class="quote-alt"><p>December 21st, 1837</p><p><strong>Eilian</strong></p></blockquote><p>The streets of New York City were colder than usual.&nbsp;</p><p>No matter how tightly Eilian pulled her shawl around her shoulders, the chill was bitter and cut her to the bone. Each step along the sidewalk was painful and slow. She was unaccompanied, which was unusual for the woman, though she knew exactly where she was headed. Even without the company of another, she knew <em>every </em>step. She wouldn’t stop until she arrived and made things right.&nbsp;</p><p><em>“Josephine?”</em></p><p><em>Eilian’s voice was soft as she rolled over in bed to find the other side empty of the one she loved. It wasn’t uncommon to wake up after Josephine had climbed from the bed in search of breakfast or to bury her nose in a novel. Her head pounded and her memory of the night before was hazy. Had she gone to bed ill? She didn’t remember.&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>“Josephine, where are you-”&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>The piece of parchment on the pillow beside her made her pause. She stared at it for several moments as she took in the handwriting she recognized as Josephine’s. The swooping, cursive letters were shakier than usual. They were smudged and the paper was distorted with what looked like dried tears. Each written word filled in the blanks in her memory and made her realize that something had gone horribly, horribly wrong.&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>“Josephine…”</em></p><p>Eilian pulled herself back to the present as she stepped around a corner. Several homeless men called out to the woman, though she paid them no attention. She pressed forward along the path. Her destination wasn’t far, though each step felt like it was an impossible distance to travel in her despair.&nbsp;</p><p>It was beginning to snow. For a moment, the Fae paused and looked up to the sky. On a normal day, in a typical moment, Eilian would have been thrilled. She might have even stuck her tongue out to catch the large flakes. On a normal, typical day, her Josephine would have been at her side.&nbsp;</p><p>But today was neither normal <em>or </em>typical.&nbsp;</p><p>The glove on her hand covered the ring she now wore on her left hand. It was golden, with shimmering diamonds that sparkled in the light. For a long time after she woke, she stared at it. She could imagine the conversation that resulted in it’s placement on her hand, though she had no memory of it. Eilian had known Jackson had fantasies of their betrothal. Eilian had been trying to figure out if she could say yes to the man she felt so drawn to without breaking the heart of the woman she <em>knew </em>to be her soulmate.&nbsp;</p><p>In the end, it seemed she had failed.&nbsp;</p><p>Eilian held no memory of the proposal, and yet she woke to a diamond on her hand and a letter that detailed the heartbreak she had given to Josephine. In the letter, her love asked her not to come for her. In each step she took now, she broke the request. No matter what, Eilian couldn’t keep herself away. Eilian would <em>always </em>fight for Josephine, even if she had cut her deeply to the bone in a decision she didn’t remember making.&nbsp;</p><p>It wasn’t the first time Eilian found herself unable to remember her own actions, though certainly the most recent. There had been little moments over the centuries: animals that were no longer injured, conversations to move them from their current cities, and most importantly, the creation of Fae themselves.&nbsp;</p><p>Long ago, Eilian realized there was something larger than herself. It was something that compelled <em>her </em>to do its bidding, though she certainly didn’t know what it was. Eilian was no ordinary young woman. She was born of stardust and goddesses, and she was meant for big, beautiful things.&nbsp;</p><p>Even at the cost of her own heart and happiness.&nbsp;</p><p><em>Josephine, I’m so sorry.&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>You asked for one thing, and I failed you.&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>You, my dear heart, are the one I want.&nbsp;</em></p><p>Eilian hadn’t meant for any of this to happen. For centuries, her heart had belonged entirely to Josephine. As far as she was concerned, it never needed to change. Something, a force she couldn’t name, had decided on a future entirely different for her.&nbsp;</p><p><em>On that warm, July afternoon, she had been out for a stroll along the Hudson River. New York City was many things, and she enjoyed the ability to bleed into the crowds, but she missed nature. Many of the women that day had parasols to hide from the bright sun, but Eilian tilted her head up to let the rays warm her skin.&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>“You’re not like the other women here, are you?”&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>His voice pulled her to him, as if she was drawn by a tether. Even as she wanted, with all her heart, to have Josephine near her, in that moment it was the Hunter that stood before her that had her full attention.&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>“I’m so sorry, that was a horrible way to start an introduction.” He dipped himself into a mock bow. His laugh made her smile, despite herself. It was musical, and she was hypnotized. “I’m Jackson Aldersley, may I have the pleasure of your name?”&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>She wanted to excuse herself. She wanted to walk away and find her way back to her home. Even with everything Eilian wanted to do, all she could do was stay in her place and take his outstretched hand. Her eyes followed as he lightly kissed the back of her knuckles. The touch was familiar, but it wasn’t her Josephine. Even then, it was something she couldn’t deny.&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>“Elyse,” Eilian murmured as she met his deep blue eyes. They reminded her of the night sky. Her name, her true name, was something they had all decided to conceal. Hunters, just like the one before her now, wanted her and her kind dead, and she had to protect herself. “Elyse Brandon.”&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>“It is a pleasure to meet you, Elyse.”</em>&nbsp;</p><p>From that moment, the relationship that bloomed was beyond her own control. Even as her heart ached for <em>Josephine</em>, she met again and again with Jackson Aldersley. Slowly, a piece of her fell in love with him. A piece of her heart was his, though the majority of it was, and would forever be, <em>hers.&nbsp;</em></p><p>Josephine had been so patient with her. It was far more than she deserved. The only thing she asked was the one thing Eilian hadn’t given her: for her to talk to her before she accepted any proposal. She had every intention of doing just that, but…&nbsp;</p><p>Fate had other plans.&nbsp;</p><p>Eilian raised her hand to knock on the door to the brownstone home. Her knock was heavy, and she pulled her shawl further around herself as she stepped back to wait for the answer on the other side. Her eyes fluttered closed. Snow stuck to her hair and her clothing, but she didn’t move from the porch.&nbsp;</p><p>“Eilian?”&nbsp;</p><p>She opened her eyes and smiled at the sight of the redheaded Witch who opened the door. Though she <em>shouldn’t </em>be the one answering the door, she wasn’t surprised to see Anna within Quinton’s home. Though, officially, Eilian knew nothing, one look at the woman told her of the child growing within her. They were unmarried, and she knew what society would say, but Eilian only felt joy for the couple.</p><p>“Hi, Anna,” Eilian murmured, running her fingers through her hair. “Is Josephine here?”&nbsp;</p><p>“I-”&nbsp;</p><p>“I’ve got it.”&nbsp;</p><p>Anna’s words were cut off by the appearance of Quinton, and the instant his green eyes landed on her, she knew the answer to her question. Josephine was inside. Josephine was <em>here.&nbsp;</em></p><p>“Your Majesty,” Quinton said as he respectfully ducked his head. He leaned to press a gentle kiss to Anna’s lips. “Go back to your lunch, my love, and I’ll be with you soon.”&nbsp;</p><p>Eilian was silent as she watched Quinton step onto the porch with her. Snow immediately began to stick in his dark brown hair. Behind him, the door was closed. The message was clear: Josephine was here, but Eilian wouldn’t be seeing her. As she held his gaze, the Fae Queen didn’t see her Knight. She only saw Josephine’s twin brother.&nbsp;</p><p>In this moment, she realized, his loyalty wasn’t to her.&nbsp;</p><p>“Quinton…”&nbsp;</p><p>“Eilian.”&nbsp;</p><p>“Is she here?”&nbsp;</p><p>“You know she is, Eilian…”&nbsp;</p><p>Quinton reached up to run his fingers through his brown hair. Here, in Brooklyn, it was impossible to distinguish him from the other wealthy men that walked the streets. He blended in so well, just as he was meant to, but she saw the boy she knew best: the one who danced with them in the meadow and loved his sister with unbreakable loyalty.&nbsp;</p><p>“I <em>need</em> to see her, Quinton,” Eilian murmured. She was cold, nearly shivering, but she kept her voice steady. She wanted to be the Fae Queen that could command a room with a look, but right now she had <em>never </em>felt more human. She had <em>never </em>felt so broken and vulnerable.&nbsp;</p><p>“Eilian, she doesn’t want to see you<em>.”&nbsp;</em></p><p>“<em>Please.</em>”&nbsp;</p><p>Her voice was desperate, and the single word made her voice crack. It mirrored the small crack in her heart that began the moment she saw the letter on the pillow. The fracture was becoming bigger now. Soon, her heart would shatter into a million pieces.&nbsp;</p><p><em>And who will you be then, Eilian?</em></p><p>“She’s hurt,” Quinton murmured. “She said you accepted his proposal.”&nbsp;</p><p>At her shiver, finally from the cold that bore all the way down to her bone, he pulled his coat off. He wrapped it around her shoulders. It protected her from the wind, but Eilian was quickly realizing the cold that ravaged her was coming from within. No amount of clothing could protect her from it.&nbsp;</p><p>“I did.”&nbsp;</p><p>“Why?” He asked softly. “Why would you do that?”&nbsp;</p><p><em>I don’t know.&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>She is the only one I want.</em></p><p><em>I don’t remember.&nbsp;</em></p><p>The voice in her head, her <em>real </em>voice, was quiet. It was almost like it wasn’t there at all. Her <em>real </em>wants and her <em>real </em>desires were overpowered by the other voice. She didn’t recognize it, though the words that left her lips next were in <em>her </em>voice, even if it didn’t feel like her at all.&nbsp;</p><p>“I love him.”&nbsp;</p><p><em>No, I don’t.</em></p><p><em>I love her.&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>What are you doing, Eilian?</em></p><p>“And Josephine? Do you love Josephine?” He asked.&nbsp;</p><p>Quinton crossed his arms across his chest. He was shivering. Though she wanted to offer his coat back, she knew he wouldn’t take it. Even if he was protecting Josephine now, he was a Knight through and through. Eilian could name none as loyal as him. There wasn’t a single soul she trusted more, with the exception of his twin sister.&nbsp;</p><p>“I love her endlessly. She is everything to me.”&nbsp;</p><p>Those words were her own. For a moment, Eilian hoped she had her own voice back. For a few blissful seconds, she thought she might be able to salvage this. Her heart ached for Josephine, and she could think of nothing she wanted more than to be wrapped in her embrace.&nbsp;</p><p>“Are you going to go through with this? Are you <em>really </em>going to marry that Hunter, my Queen?” Quinton asked, shaking his head slightly.&nbsp;</p><p><em>Of course not, </em>Eilian thought, but those weren’t the words that left her lips.&nbsp;</p><p>“I am,” the voice that both was and wasn’t her own stated. “I love him, too.”&nbsp;</p><p><em>No, I don’t!&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>Please, Quinton.&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>Help me, I don’t want to do this.&nbsp;</em></p><p>“I don’t understand,” Quinton whispered.&nbsp;</p><p><em>I don’t either.&nbsp;</em></p><p>As she met them, she saw the pain in his deep green eyes. Eilian wondered if it even began to mirror the pain in the one she loved. Sometimes, she questioned if Quinton and Josephine could feel each other’s pain. Their bond was exceptional. She had never seen anything like it, not even in the bond she had with Saoirse.&nbsp;</p><p>“Please, Quinton. I <em>need </em>to see her. I <em>need </em>to-”&nbsp;</p><p>“Eilian, please don’t ask me to choose between my loyalty to my Queen and my loyalty to my sister.”&nbsp;</p><p>His words cut her to the core. The cold at her center flared, and Eilian recognized it now for what it was: pure, ruthless heartbreak. She closed her eyes and took a breath. Everything inside of her felt as if it was dying. Eilian was breaking, and in her place rose Elyse Brandon, soon to be Elyse Aldersley. The persona meant to protect her was the one that was shattering her.&nbsp;</p><p>“I can… explain,” She whispered. Her gaze broke from his and she stared at the ground. Snow was beginning to stick to the pavement. In another life, she would have been overjoyed.</p><p>“Explain what, my Queen?”&nbsp;</p><p>Quinton reached to touch her arm. His voice was gentle. When she looked up, he opened his arms and she stepped into them. She rested her head against his chest and let herself sink into him. She should have found safety in her Knight’s embrace, but she felt <em>anything </em>but comfort. Her ache only grew.&nbsp;</p><p>“I…”&nbsp;</p><p>“May I be blunt, Your Majesty?”&nbsp;</p><p>“Of course…”&nbsp;</p><p>“If you go in there, you’re going to ask her to stay, aren’t you?”&nbsp;</p><p>He paused only long enough for Eilian’s nod. Of <em>course </em>she would ask her to stay. She wanted to be with her. Eternally. Endlessly. It had been their plan. Forever, and then some. Their love was vast and everlasting. It was written in the very universe.&nbsp;</p><p>Or at least, she thought it was.&nbsp;</p><p>“And if you ask, she’ll stay. Josephine would do anything for you, and you know that.”&nbsp;</p><p>“I know-”&nbsp;</p><p>“So she would stay, and watch as you marry him. She would watch as you built a life with him. Maybe even have children. She would stay, silently at your side, and with every passing second, my sister would slip away. She would stay for her love for you, and completely lose herself in the process. Eilian, I know you love her, but I don’t think you see how much you’ve <em>shattered </em>her over the last year.”&nbsp;</p><p><em>Shattered. Just like you.&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>I didn’t mean to-</em></p><p><em>But you did.&nbsp;</em></p><p>“Please,” Quinton murmured. His voice was barely louder than the falling snow. In it, she heard every bit of love he held for her. It was nearly as immense as her own. “If you love her, Eilian, you have two choices.”&nbsp;</p><p><em>Two choices.&nbsp;</em></p><p>“Marry him and let her go, or end it with him and choose her.”&nbsp;</p><p><em>That’s an easy choice.&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>It’s her.&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>It has always been her.&nbsp;</em></p><p>“I’m going to marry him,” the voice said. It sounded like her, but it wasn’t <em>her</em>. It wasn’t what <em>she</em> wanted. Despite that, she had no choice. Something larger than herself had chosen <em>him</em>.&nbsp;</p><p>Elyse over Eilian.</p><p>Jackson over Josephine.&nbsp;</p><p>“Then, Eilian, you have to let her go.”&nbsp;</p><p>Eilian looked to the door. She knew she could command him to let her in. She knew she could barge in and demand to see <em>her</em> Josephine. She could order her to stay at her side. Eilian knew she would. If Eilian asked, Josephine would.&nbsp;</p><p><em>But it would break her.&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>I know, but I can’t live without her.&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>Would you choose your own comfort over her happiness?&nbsp;</em></p><p>Eilian closed her eyes. Tears streamed down her face. Inside, she was raging to be <em>Eilian </em>and choose <em>Josephine</em>, but she had no choice in the matter. Something she couldn’t control or understand was compelling her. She couldn’t disobey, even if she desperately wanted to.&nbsp;</p><p>She stepped back from Quinton and out of his arms. She didn’t raise her gaze to look at him as she pulled the coat from around her shoulders. She pressed it into his hands before she turned and made her way back down the steps to the sidewalk. Internally, Eilian was <em>screaming</em>, but her body guided her away.&nbsp;</p><p><em>You have to marry him. You have no choice.&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>If I explain-</em></p><p><em>If you ask her to stay, you’ll only cause her pain.&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>But-&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>Let her go, Eilian.&nbsp;</em></p><p>“Your Majesty-”&nbsp;</p><p>Quinton’s voice pulled her from her thoughts. He had followed her down the steps and placed his hand on her arm to stop her. Tears were pouring down her face, and her chest was heaving with each gasping sob that left her lips. Her body didn’t feel like her own.&nbsp;</p><p>“Eilian, let me-”&nbsp;</p><p>“Elyse.”&nbsp;</p><p>“What?”&nbsp;</p><p>“It’s… Elyse.”&nbsp;</p><p>Once more, the voice that both was and wasn’t her own spoke with a confidence she didn’t feel. She was both sobbing and speaking like a Queen. She couldn’t be sure which was the real her, if the real her existed at all.&nbsp;</p><p>“Elyse… Aldersley.”&nbsp;</p><p>“My Queen-”&nbsp;</p><p>“I’m alright. I’ll be alright,” Elyse whispered as she looked up to meet his eyes again. Right now, she wished he had Josephine’s eyes. She wished she could see them one last time. She wished she could say goodbye.&nbsp;</p><p>“Let me walk you home-”&nbsp;</p><p>“No,” Elyse reached out to put her hand on his arm. “Your duty is to her right now. Take care of her.”&nbsp;</p><p>“I will, my Queen.”</p><p>“Good,” Elyse squeezed his arm before she stepped back. “And Quinton?”</p><p>“Yes?”&nbsp;</p><p>“Congratulations,” She murmured. Despite her tears, she managed to smile through them. “On the baby. He’ll be healthy, and so strong.”&nbsp;</p><p><em>Please, please don’t go.&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>I have to.&nbsp;</em></p><p><em>No, not without her-</em></p><p><em>Let her go.&nbsp;</em></p><p>Elyse stepped back away from him. With one last glance to his eyes, she turned and started down the sidewalk. Inside of her, Eilian crumbled and broke. On the outside, it didn’t matter.&nbsp;</p><p>She was no longer the girl from the meadow who created constellations to light up the sky just so she could see the way they reflected in Josephine’s eyes. She was no longer the woman who danced with Josephine in the tall grass and decorated her hair with flower crowns. No matter what, she would carry Josephine and her love with her, but she was no longer Eilian. She would never be again.&nbsp;</p><p>She was Elyse Brandon, soon to be Aldersley, just as the universe wanted.&nbsp;</p><p>And she hated every last second.&nbsp;</p>
