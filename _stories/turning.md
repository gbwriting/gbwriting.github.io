  <p>November 4th, 1994</p>
  <p><strong>Andie</strong></p>
</blockquote>

<p>Hours ago, there had been a sort of confidence when it came to what she was doing, but that confidence was quickly fading as she watched Tamzin’s heart begin to slow. Her voice was shaky as she managed each word and carefully marked Tamzin’s fair skin with runes written in her own blood. With each word, Andie’s eyes remained locked with the woman under her: the one she loved endlessly and completely. </p><p>The one, she hoped with every piece of her, who would wake up from this. </p><p>The ritual was complete, and Tamzin’s gaze remained on Andie’s. She was terrified to move or speak. Every last bit of her was seized with a terror she’d never known before. Each passing second was marked with the steady tick of the clock above Tamzin’s desk. </p><p><em>Tick. Tick. Tick. </em></p><p><em>What if I did it wrong? </em></p><p><em>Tick. Tick. Tick.</em></p><p><em>What if I just killed the only person who has ever loved me? </em></p><p><em>Tick. Tick. Tick. </em></p><p><em>What if this was all for nothing? </em></p><p><em>Tick. Tick. Tick. </em></p><p>Andie wished there was some indication the ritual had worked, but she knew there would be nothing until Tamzin woke up. Or, a more horrifying thought, if she didn’t. Tamzin’s eyes had fluttered closed. Her body was limp. Andie sucked in a breath and held it as she watched Tamzin take one last breath in her human body. Then, there was nothing. There was no breathing, no heartbeat, and no sign of life in the woman she loved. </p><p><em>Tick. Tick. Tick. </em></p><p><em>Tamzin, I’m afraid. </em></p><p><em>Tick. Tick. Tick.</em></p><p><em>Tamzin, I can’t do this without you. </em></p><p><em>Tick. Tick. Tick. </em></p><p>Andie knew she needed to move. Her son slept in the next room. When Tamzin had initially moved into her flat, Andie presumed the second bedroom would be an office or a studio. Instead, she transformed it into a bedroom for Sawyer. Painted elephants, tigers, and lions decorated the walls and chased away his bad dreams. Tonight, those paintings were his protectors while his mother - his <em>mothers - </em>did the ritual that would make Tamzin immortal. </p><p><em>Tick. Tick. Tick. </em></p><p><em>Were you as afraid as I am when I stopped breathing? </em></p><p><em>Tick. Tick. Tick.</em></p><p><em>How did you possibly survive that fear? </em></p><p><em>Tick. Tick. Tick. </em></p><p>Andie forced herself to her feet. Her own turning was less than a year ago. The pain was something she remembered often. Over the last few months, each time Tamzin brought up the possibility of being turned herself, the memories would flood her. With each one came absolute panic. For months, Andie refused to consider the idea. For months, Tamzin had insisted their friendship was <em>forever. </em></p><p>It took everything Andie had to move into the bathroom. In the mirror, she caught a glimpse of herself. She was pale and shaking. Her knuckles were white as she gripped the sink basin and forced herself to take deep, deep breaths. It would be okay. Even if pessimism and doubt gripped at her, she forced herself to breathe. Even if Tamzin didn’t wake up, she had to breathe. She had promised, even if she doubted she would be able to keep it in the event Tamzin didn’t wake up. </p><p><em>“Andie, come here.” </em></p><p><em>The memory of her voice crept into her mind as she gripped the porcelain. Her ability played the moment in perfect clarity right down to the smell of Tamzin’s perfume and the acidic taste in her mouth from the vomit she hadn’t told her about. She was shaking just as much in the memory as she was now. </em></p><p><em>“Andromeda, my love.” </em></p><p><em>Arms wrapped around her. In the present, Andie’s mind reminded her that it could have been for the last time. Even now, she felt the gentle way Tamzin squeezed her from behind. The way Tamzin always tapped her index finger against her arm when she hugged her. Did she know she did that? Andie had never asked. Now, she worried she wouldn’t get the chance.</em></p><p><em>“I’m scared, Tamzin. What if you-” </em></p><p><em>“I will, Andie.” </em></p><p><em>Tamzin turned her in her arms and placed a hand on her cheek. Her thumb grazed lovingly across Andie’s skin just below her eye. She leaned into the touch and let herself breathe. As always, Tamzin brought her to the present. She pushed away fear with one touch. In Tamzin’s arms, Andie would forever be safe and loved. </em></p><p><em>“What did I tell you on our first night?”</em></p><p><em>“You told me,” Andie whispered as she met Tamzin’s blue eyes. She had long ago memorized each flake in them, whether they be sky blue, azure, sapphire, or periwinkle. “You told me that I had you now, and that you’re bloody hard to get rid of.” </em></p><p><em>“Exactly. I’ll come back for you, Andie. I’ll find my way back.”</em></p><p><em>“What if you-” </em></p><p><em>“Andromeda Lillian Collins,” Tamzin whispered before she cut her words off with a kiss. The touch filled Andie with electricity and warmth. With one breath, she chased away the demons. “I promise you, I will always find my way to you, no matter where you go.” </em></p><p><em>“I love you, Tamzin.” </em></p><p><em>“And I love you, Andromeda. Forever, and then some,” Tamzin stroked under her eye again. “Do you remember the ritual?”</em></p><p><em>Andie nodded. She couldn’t forget it if she tried. </em></p><p><em>“Are you ready?” </em></p><p><em>Andie wasn’t so sure she was, but Tamzin’s steady smile and confidence bled into her. Andie held the knife she would use to cut her skin in her hand while Tamzin positioned herself on the bed. The pills were washed down her throat in four handfuls. A lethal amount. At least it wouldn’t be painful. It was the one thing she insisted on. </em></p><p><em>Tamzin’s hands guided her as she positioned herself to be straddling her lap. There was no fear in Tamzin’s eyes, though it raged within Andie. Her body had already expelled their dinner and more bile threatened to come up now. Before anything else could happen, Tamzin’s hand fell to her cheek and drew her in to a kiss. It was slow and passionate. Each tiny movement of lips reminded Andie of why they were doing this: so they could be together forever, no matter what. </em></p><p><em>“I love you, Andromeda,” Tamzin whispered when she pulled back.</em></p><p><em>“And I love you, Tamzin.” </em></p><p><em>Tamzin nodded, and Andie brought the knife to her palm. She was shaking. She couldn’t do this. Just as her lips parted to voice her fear, she felt Tamzin’s fingers grip hers over the hilt of the knife. Blue eyes met hers, questioning if it was alright. Andie nodded and let Tamzin slice her skin. The scarlet of her blood dripped onto Tamzin’s shirt. She let herself meet Tamzin’s eyes once more before she dipped her finger in the blood and lifted it to paint the runes on Tamzin’s skin.</em> </p><p>“Mummy?” </p><p>Andie pulled herself from her mind with a shake of her head. The tiny voice startled her, and she turned to see Sawyer standing in the doorway of the bathroom. His eyes were wide as he took in the sight of his mother. She was covered in her own blood and tears were streaking down her face. He was <em>never </em>supposed to see this. She had failed him, and terror gripped at her heart to tell her she had failed Tamzin, too. </p><p>“Mummy, hurt?” </p><p>Before she could say anything, she felt his little hand grip at her finger. She knew what he was doing and she held her hand out to him. It wasn’t the first time he had seen her bleed, though the first from her own hand. Tiny fingers traced the line of her wound. With his touch, the cut stitched together. If it wasn’t for the dried blood, Andie would have believed it was never there at all. </p><p>“Thank you,” Andie whispered. </p><p>She knelt before him and cupped his face in her hands. In his eyes, just as green as her own, she saw understanding far beyond his years. She didn’t know if it was the Fae blood that ran through his veins, the very nature of their home life with his father, or something else entirely, but he had grown up too fast. In all of her efforts to give him a good childhood, the world seemed to want to work against her. </p><p>“Mummy Tamzin?” </p><p>Just as it always did, the way he spoke her name made Andie smile. He drew out the ‘a’ sound in Tamzin’s name each time. <em>Taaaaamzin</em>. Each time, she melted. Tamzin did too, both from the pronunciation and the title he bestowed to her entirely of his own accord. </p><p>“Mummy Tamzin is going to be okay, love. When Mummy Tamzin wakes up, she’s going to be Fae, just like you and me.” </p><p>“Just like me?” His tiny voice was so hopeful. It filled Andie with the same sort of joy. </p><p>“Just like you,” She repeated as she pressed a kiss to his temple. “Come on, darling. Let Mummy put you back to bed.” </p><p>“No,” Sawyer whispered as he clutched at her. “I stay.” </p><p>“Okay.” </p><p>Andie knew she should put him to bed, but she couldn’t find the will. Instead, he stood beside her as she dipped her hands into the sink to wash the blood from her hands. Unlike her own turning, where William had slit her wrists to kill her human body, none of Tamzin’s blood was shed. At the very least, there was that. </p><p>She gathered rags from the shelf and wet them with warm water and soap. It smelled like lavender, just like Tamzin. She inhaled to take in the scent before she reached to take her son’s hand. He didn’t protest. He never did, and Andie often wondered how she was blessed with such a sweet soul in her toddler. Where other parents complained of tantrums, Andie was blessed with a small angel. She led him into the bedroom and sat him on Tamzin’s armchair. </p><p>“Sit right here, my love. Close your eyes.” </p><p>Andie pressed a kiss to his head as his eyes fluttered closed. Long eyelashes fluttered, but he didn’t peek. She smiled and ran fingers through his hair before she stepped over to Tamzin. Each rune was meticulously scrubbed from Tamzin’s skin until there was no trace of what had been done. When she was finished, she tossed the rags into the bin. </p><p>Tamzin’s shirt followed as the Fae replaced the blood stained one with a fresh, clean one. She settled Tamzin back into the pillows and tried to make it seem as if she was simply sleeping. Her fingers lovingly brushed through her hair as she watched her. <em>I love you, Tamzin</em>, Andie silently whispered. <em>Please, please wake up soon. </em>She leaned to press a kiss to her head before she pulled her own bloody clothes off. They were disposed of and replaced by flannel pajama pants and one of Tamzin’s soft shirts. </p><p>“Mummy?” Sawyer’s tiny voice forced her gaze back to the toddler, who was still obediently sitting with his eyes closed. She went to him and lifted him into her arms. </p><p>“It’s okay, love. I’ve got you.” </p><p>She should tuck him into bed, but as his eyes opened and met her own, she couldn’t bare the thought of being apart from him. Instead, she brought him to the bed and placed him beside Tamzin. Immediately, his tiny hand came to rest on her cheek. Andie sat beside him and smiled. </p><p>“Just like me,” He repeated and leaned down to press a kiss to her cheek. “Love you, Mummy Tamzin.” </p><p>Andie smiled as she watched him lay his head on the pillows and crawled onto her chest. His tiny head rested against Tamzin’s body. In a different moment, she might have imagined they were sleeping peacefully rather than going through a turning. After all, they had already spent countless nights curled up this way. </p><p><em>I shouldn’t let him see this. </em></p><p><em>He understands it better than you do. </em></p><p><em>He’ll be traumatized. </em></p><p><em>He already is at home. </em></p><p>Andie moved to lay beside them. Her son - <em>their </em>son - was nestled between them as she pressed in close. Sawyer’s head still rested on Tamzin’s chest, and within seconds, Andie’s followed as she wrapped her arm around them both. They were her family, and she reminded herself this was all for <em>both </em>of them. In the end, when Tamzin woke up, they would keep pressing forward until the day Andie was able to flee England with Tamzin and their son. </p><p><em>Tick. Tick. Tick. </em></p><p>The sound of the clock filled her ears once more. Now, the seconds passed slightly faster as she timed them with the rise and fall of Sawyer’s breathing. His eyes fluttered closed again as sleep began to take him. Andie stretched up, pressing a kiss to his cheek before she moved to press her lips to Tamzin’s still form. </p><p><em>Tick. Tick. Tick.</em></p><p><em>Look at this, Tamzin. Our son. Our family. It’s all going to be ours. Forever. </em></p><p><em>Tick. Tick. Tick. </em></p><p><em>Come back soon, my love. We’ll be right here. </em></p>
