<blockquote data-alt="true" class="quote-alt">Saturday, April 6th, 2019<p><strong>Piper</strong></p></blockquote><p>At some point in the night, the thumping, pulsing music that had pressed an ache into her forehead faded away into nothingness. The walls still shook with the bass, but Piper couldn’t hear the melody or tell which pop song blared through the speakers downstairs. At the moment, Piper couldn’t tell much of anything. It was a feeling she was growing increasingly familiar with. Over the past three months, this feeling of bland nothingness had become her norm. Her world, which had once felt vivid, bright, and alive with endless possibilities, now felt dull, dark, and empty. </p><p>The lights in the bedroom were dim, which he had done intentionally to set the mood, but it was enough light for Piper to make out her environment. It looked like any other home in Northern Greenborough. If her life depended on it, she would be unable to identify who owned this house. The identity of the host of the party was unknown to her, or rather, she didn’t care. They didn’t matter to her. They were as meaningless as the bland, beige Pottery Barn-catalog-wannabe guest room Piper currently rested in. </p><p>“You feel so fucking good, Piper.” </p><p><em>Do I? </em></p><p>Noah’s words felt far away, just like his touches across her skin. He hadn’t bothered to remove her dress when he’d laid her down on the dusty blue duvet. He’d only pulled her underwear from her and pushed her skirts up and out of the way before he’d taken her.</p><p>His touches barely registered on her skin. Five months ago, they felt as bewitching as if he was the prince of a favorite fairy tale. Then, she had imagined <em>him </em>in all of the books she read. Each elfish prince, scandalous pirate, and runaway prince had <em>his </em>face. Their love had been magical then. To him, it still was. </p><p><em>I’m sorry I’m not the girl I was, Noah.</em></p><p><em>It’s not your fault that I’m broken now. </em></p><p><em>How do you still love this ghost of a girl? </em></p><p>Piper wasn’t even aware he had finished, nor that he had pulled out of her, until he shifted his weight off of her. Blue eyes fluttered open as he stood and walked across the room into the bathroom to clean himself. She felt his absence as if it were a string that pulled at her sanity and unraveled her bit by bit. Her lungs felt tighter, and the anxiety flared within her even more. </p><p><em>I’m sorry. </em></p><p><em>Oh god, Noah. </em></p><p><em>I’m so sorry. </em></p><p><em>You deserve better than this. </em></p><p><em>I’m so sorry. </em></p><p>Piper didn’t realize she was crying until she tasted her tears. The salty taste on her tongue mixed with the lingering taste of the beer she’d gulped down out of the red, plastic cup half an hour before, or the minty taste of Noah’s preferred mints, which always lingered on his tongue in each kiss. She didn’t move from her place on the bed, though she did reach up to press the palms of her hands against her eyes. </p><p>“Piper?” </p><p>His voice was gentle, and she opened her eyes to meet his bright green gaze. Noah was kneeling beside the bed. Somehow, she had turned on her side and was curled in on herself. Her body was full of tension as she held herself in the fetal position. She held herself so tightly as if she were afraid she might completely come undone otherwise. </p><p>It hadn’t always been like this. Three months ago, it felt like all Piper did was smile. Then, <em>before</em>, she would be the life of the party on nights like these. She would stand next to Riley and let molly dissolve on their tongues <em>together. </em>She’d laugh with her best friend for hours, then press herself against her boyfriend and feel his hips against her own as they danced to the same pulsing music she now tried to drown out. </p><p><em>Before</em>, when she would sneak off to fuck Noah in the bland, Pottery Barn-wannabe bedrooms, she would be an active participant. She would hold herself firmly to him, or coax him into holding her tightly against the wall with her legs around his waist. In those encounters, she was an active participant who kissed her boyfriend and relished in their mutual, shared pleasure. </p><p>Now, she was a shadow of a girl. Even now, when she had been the one to pull him to the bedroom, she couldn’t wrap her mind around their kisses or focus on the way he felt inside of her. She couldn’t feel anything, not even him, but <em>god</em>, did she feel the absence of him. </p><p>“<em>Noah</em>,” Piper managed. Her voice cracked on his name. The sound only intensified the intensity of her sobs. No tears left her eyes now, though her body shook with each heave of her lungs. “Noah, I can’t-” </p><p>“Did I hurt you?” He asked. </p><p>“No.” </p><p><em>The world hurt me, Noah. </em></p><p>Noah crawled over her to settle behind her in the bed. His arms came to snake around her middle and he pulled her back tightly against his chest. Piper gripped at his forearms to try to pull him closer. She wanted to disappear into him. She felt the way he turned his face into her neck as he held her close. The peppered kisses along her skin helped to ground her, and she clutched at him even tighter. </p><p>“I’ve got you, babe.” </p><p><em>I don’t deserve you.</em></p><p>“Don’t let me go,” She pleaded. </p><p>“Never. I’m right here.” </p><p><em>You could have anyone else. </em></p><p><em>I’m not worth this. </em></p><p>Piper wished she could be anything but this. She hated the way she was gripping onto him. She felt like a desperate, pitiful thing. Three months ago, Piper would have laughed at the kind of girl who sobbed like this in their boyfriend’s arms. Now, she was shattered beyond measure, and Noah was the <em>one </em>thing that made sense. </p><p>Once, they had all been whole. Just three months ago, they celebrated her sister’s birthday in a grand, rented out hall. Piper had been giddy when she took turns with Riley twirling her niece, Alice, in grand circles on the dance floor. She had toasted endless flutes of champagne with Percy and gorged herself on chocolate covered strawberries with Ben. That night, just three short months ago, her world had been so, so bright. </p><p>Then, barely three weeks after that beautiful night, a rogue Hunter decided to open fire at the Greenborough Winter Carnival, and they lost Ben. He wasn’t Piper’s father, but he might as well have been. She had more memories with him than she had with her own. With his dying breath, the lights went out in <em>everyone </em>she loved. </p><p>Riley lost herself to drugs, alcohol, or her new girlfriend Amber nearly every night. Bailey was still in the hospital following her suicide attempt. Sydney still spent most days curled up in her bedroom hunched over a sketchbook. Percy barely got out of bed or answered her calls. Her mother spent most nights with Saoirse to hold her while she slept. With one gunshot, Piper’s big, bright world lost all its light. </p><p>Noah was the only thing in her life that wasn’t broken, too. </p><p>“I <em>love</em> you.” </p><p>Piper’s voice was desperate as she turned her body into Noah’s chest. Her arms wound tightly around his middle. Her fingers wound tightly in the fabric of his shirt, and she clung to him as if he were the only thing keeping her from fading away. In some ways, she wondered if he was. </p><p>“I love you too, Piper,” He whispered, pressing his lips to the top of her head. “I love you. I’ve got you.” </p><p>“Please, <em>please</em>.” </p><p>“I’m right here, babe. I’ve got you.” </p><p>His hands were rubbing her back, and she sank into the touch as she clutched even tighter to him. There were no more tears being shed, but her quick, labored breathing was still making her body heave and shake in his arms. </p><p>“You deserve-” </p><p>“None of that,” He whispered. “You’re safe.” </p><p>“I love you.” </p><p>“I love you, too. I’ve got you.” </p><p>“Please, don’t leave me.” </p><p>Piper’s voice was desperate. She pressed herself even tighter to him. She wanted to make them one singular being. If she lost herself in him and <em>his </em>light, it didn’t matter that she no longer had her own. Darkness was all around her and slowly eating away everything and everyone she loved. </p><p>“Never, Piper. I’m not going anywhere. I’ve got you.” </p><p>Noah was the only light she knew, and she didn’t want to let him go. </p><p>She <em>couldn’t </em>let him go. </p><p>Even if he deserved so, so much better than <em>her</em>.</p>
