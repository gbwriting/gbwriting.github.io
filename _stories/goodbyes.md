<blockquote class="quote-alt"><p>February 1850</p><p><strong>Josephine</strong></p></blockquote>

<p>This was the last place that Jo wanted to be, but it was where she had to be. Quinton was gone. Her brother. Her rock. Her moon. Her other half. It was a time that she hoped would never come, especially after she lost her wife only a few months before. Lilly. The ache was still there and it was only getting stronger with everything she lost. Jo didn’t know what it would mean now that Quinton was gone. She didn’t know what would become of her, and she especially didn’t know what would happen to Hendrix. Her nephew would be living with her, and she would have to continue the incredible work that her brother had already done.</p><p>Jo wasn’t sure she could do it, but she loved the boy and would do her best. She knew that traditionally a Fae child would live with their Protector if they lost their parents. It was an option that was given to Hendrix, but it was one that he declined. Jo didn’t fight it, and would willingly take him back to England with her. He had spent the majority of his life there already, so it only made sense that he would want to stay with her. They had always had a close bond, but she couldn’t help but wonder if Quinton’s death would affect that. As it was, Jo already knew that she was no longer the same woman she was months ago. There was too much loss and too much heartbreak, so what remained was a broken woman hiding behind a facade of strength.</p><p>So there Jo stood at her brother’s grave with so many questions in her mind and no answers for any of them. The casket hadn’t been lowered into the ground yet. Jo insisted that she needed a moment alone before Quinton was lowered into his final resting place. Her dark eyes were fixed on the vessel that held her brother’s body. Jo felt numb, and she wondered if that was how she would go through life. Numb for eternity, or at least until a Hunter took her from the world just as one took her brother.</p><p>Quinton died an honorable death, she knew that, but that didn’t make it hurt any less. It didn’t give Hendrix a father or a mother. All it gave the boy was an aunt who couldn’t feel anything. He deserved better than that, and yet she knew that he would never leave her side. They were all that was left of their family. Everyone else was dead, and they all died when they tried to protect the ones they loved. Jo knew it was only a matter of time before that was her fate. That seemed to be their family’s legacy, dying honorable deaths to protect the ones they loved.</p><p>“I am so sorry, Quinton. It should have been me. Hendrix needs you. I need you. <em>She</em> needs you.” </p><p>Jo couldn’t say her name, but she knew that Quinton would know who she meant. Eilian, their Queen, the woman Jo would always love and the one she would never have again. Tears burned Jo’s eyes but she refused to let them fall. If she cried she would never stop, so instead she would be numb.</p><p><em>“Bloody hell, Josephine. I thought you were taught better than that! Mum would be so disappointed in you.” </em></p><p><em>Quinton’s voice was loud, joyful, and filled with enviable dramatics. He was teasing her about the dinner she had been preparing. It was going perfectly until her wife distracted her as she so often did. The bread had burned and the soup was burned and stuck to the pot. It was edible, but it was far from the best thing she had made.</em></p><p><em>“You’re a terror.”</em></p><p><em>Jo’s voice was teasing as she walked along the pond. It was a beautiful day, one of those rare English summers where the sun was shining and they were filled with warmth. It was perfect, but any time spent with her family was perfect to her. Lilly was walking with Hendrix’s hand in hers several feet back. She knew the trouble that Jo and her brother could get into together. The twins were a force, and together they could do anything. Today, what they chose to do was torture each other as siblings so often did.</em></p><p><em>“I may be a terror, but at least I can cook.”</em></p><p><em>Jo shot him a look before she continued on her walk along the water. To anyone else it would have looked like a peaceful stroll, but Lilly knew better. When Quinton fell into step beside her, he nudged her playfully with his shoulder.</em></p><p><em>“Buck up, little bit. You can’t be good at everything.”</em></p><p><em>“Can’t I?”</em></p><p><em>Without warning Jo’s hands slammed against Quinton’s shoulders. She had caught him off guard just as she wanted. This was only further proved when he lost his footing and fell into the water. Jo’s dark eyes shimmered with mischief and pride. She had bested her brother, or at least she thought she did. Before she knew what was happening she felt a firm push from behind and she went tumbling into the water right beside Quinton. When Jo pushed herself back to her feet she spun on the duo who blindsided her. A playfully stern look was given before a bright smile took it’s place.</em></p><p><em>“Aren’t you supposed to be on my side, Lilly? You’re my wife.” Jo turned on Hendrix the mischief shimmering in her eyes again. “And you little, you little demon aren’t supposed to help her.”</em></p><p><em>Lilly and Hendrix were both laughing. It was the loud joyous laughter that showed how truly happy they were together. They had bested Jo which is something that could never be done. Jo crossed her arms over her chest and glared at her wife and nephew who were doubled over laughing at the state she was in. Then with one glance back at her brother, Jo knew that he was now on her side and together they pulled Hendrix and Lilly into the pond with them.</em></p><p>Jo was pulled from the memory when she felt the gentle press of fingers intertwining with hers. It was a touch she would forever know, even if she wanted to forget it and the pain that came with it. Eilian. She meant to pull her hand away, but instead she felt her fingers tighten their grip to the Queen’s hand.</p><p>The last time they spoke was at Quinton and Anna’s wedding, and it was a conversation she wished she could forget. Eilian had tried to use her command as a Queen to keep Jo from leaving to England. It didn’t work, Jo still left when the wedding was over. Quinton teleported her to England where she created her own home in solitude. At least, that was the intention, but Quinton would never let her be alone for long. He would often teleport to see her, and when Hendrix was born he would bring him along. Jo started being pieced back together little by little, and then she met Lilly who helped sew her back together. Now they were all gone, and all that was left of her family was her nephew.</p><p>“I’m sorry, Josephine.” Eilian’s voice was quiet and unsure, two things that were never associated with the strong Fae Queen.</p><p>“He was a Knight, he knew what it meant.”</p><p>“That doesn’t make it easier.”</p><p>Dark eyes finally shifted from the casket to focus on the woman at her side. She was just as beautiful as she always had been. Jo couldn’t help but take a few seconds to take in every detail of the woman she loved and could never have again. Sadness rest deep in the Queen’s beautiful blue eyes. Eyes that she could get lost in. Eyes that were her undoing.</p><p>She wasn’t sure how long they simply stood there looking into each other’s eyes. There was so much said in the silence. Movement from the corner of her eye caught her attention and Eilian’s hand came to rest gently on Jo’s cheek. Jo leaned into the touch, and every it of her ached. The touch that once filled her with so much warmth now filled her with so much pain. It was Eilian though, and she would always long for her Queen’s touch.</p><p>“Josephine…”</p><p>The whisper of her name on Eilian’s lips sounded like a prayer that was for her alone. It made her stomach flutter, a feeling she had long thought dead. She felt the woman she had loved for countless years step closer. They press of their bodies together sent electricity through her.</p><p>“Eilian, I-”</p><p>Jo’s words were cut off with the press of Eilian’s lips to hers. It felt like fire and electricity and a million other wonderful things. It was the first thing she had truly felt since Hendrix arrived at her home with Quinton’s body. Her heartbeat quickened as their lips and tongues moved together in a dance that only they knew. Jo was falling deeper and deeper into the kiss. She was letting herself disappear into Eilian as she had done so many times before. A quiet whimper fell from her lips and was met with the soft whisper of her name.</p><p>“Josephine, my sweet Josephine.”</p>
