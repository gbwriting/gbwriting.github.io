Welcome to Greenborough, Oregon!

Founded in 1844, Greenborough, Oregon is known for being the first city in the world to provide full protection and rights to its supernatural citizens. Over the years, it’s become a cultural hub that’s nestled along the coast of the Pacific Northwest that draws in people from across the globe.

Greenborough was created in April 2022 by Kaci & Sarah Loveless-Hall.
