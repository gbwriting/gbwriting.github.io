Districts & Key Locations
North

Northern Greenborough

This area of Greenborough is known for being home to the most affluent families of the city. Here, you can find larger homes, private schools, and elite shopping districts. As we get closer to the city's center, the architecture transitions to luxurious high rise apartments.

Northeast

Northeastern Greenborough

This area is defined by upper-middle class neighborhoods - full of tree lined streets, spacious family homes, excellent schools, and high-end shopping centers. As you travel further east, the suburban neighborhoods gradually give way to dense forests.

Downtown

Meadow View Park

Inspired by iconic urban green spaces, Meadow View Park serves as downtown's primary outdoor space. This park is central to the city and features expansive lawns, a permanent pavillion for music, a winter ice skating rink, and much more.
