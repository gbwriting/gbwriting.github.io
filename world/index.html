Districts & Key Locations
North

Northern Greenborough

This area of Greenborough is known for being home to the most affluent families of the city. Here, you can find larger homes, private schools, and elite shopping districts. As we get closer to the city's center, the architecture transitions to luxurious high rise apartments.

Northeast

Northeastern Greenborough

This area is defined by upper-middle class neighborhoods - full of tree lined streets, spacious family homes, excellent schools, and high-end shopping centers. As you travel further east, the suburban neighborhoods gradually give way to dense forests.

Downtown

Meadow View Park

Inspired by iconic urban green spaces, Meadow View Park serves as downtown's primary outdoor space. This park is central to the city and features expansive lawns, a permanent pavillion for music, a winter ice skating rink, and much more.

Downtown

Downtown Greenborough

Downtown Greenborough serves as the urban core of the city. This area is defined by towering high rises, corporate headquarters, and government buildings. Ground floor storefronts and restaurants sit beneath residential apartments, creating a highly walkable urban area at the heart of the city.

Northwest

Northwest Beach

Located along the northern coast, this area of Greenborough blends coastal living with local attractions. Lined with beachfront housing, the city aquarium, and the Greenborough Pier, it’s a popular location for locals and tourists alike.

Northwest

The Pier

Known as one of the most iconic locations in Greenborough, The Pier is a waterfront landmark that is packed with classic attractions, arcade halls, local eateries, and local vendors. It’s a favorite place to go for entertainment, drawing large crowds day and night.

West

Western Greenborough

Positioned along the central coast between the beach regions, this area of Greenborough manages the city’s maritime trade and shipping operations. While primarily industrial with warehouses, docks, and clean harbor infrastructure, quiet pockets of residential housing sit tucked along its outer edges.

East

Eastern Greenborough

Eastern Greenborough is known as a cultural hub for it’s residents. It’s a well established region with comfortable residential housing, local shopping malls, and public schools. Here, there’s a good mix of walkable, residential neighborhoods and sprawling neighborhoods.

South

Greenborough University

Greenborough University is an elite institution that is known for its academics as much as its beautiful campus. The campus is filled with historical architecture and walkable green spaces, and features state-of-the-art libraries, performing arts halls, academic buildings, and an iconic quad.

Southwest

University District

Situated between the university and the southern coast, this vibrant neighborhood is young and trendy — perfect for student life. The streets are filled with artsy boutiques, indie coffee shops, local pubs, and many off-campus housing options.

Southwest

Southwest Beach

The southern shoreline of Greenborough is home to an artsy, energetic atmosphere. Unlike the tourist filled northern beaches, this area tends to be bustling with students and is filled with local bars, artsy kiosks, and plenty of space to enjoy the water.

Southeast

Southeastern Greenborough

Southeastern Greenborough gives way to quiet, residential neighborhoods. Here, you’ll find suburban neighborhoods, public parks, and a quieter vibe. It’s also home to Greenborough University’s primary athletic complexes, as well as the University’s hospital system.
